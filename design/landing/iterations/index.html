Everywhere you've been. Everywhere you'll go. Completely free.

Drop your Strava archive and every route you've ever logged lights up as one heatmap, read entirely in your browser.

Drop your export .zip
GPX, TCX or FIT
No login · No upload · No server

Your data never leaves your machine.

There's no account and no upload. When you drop your archive, Freeroam unzips and parses every GPS track right here in the browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, because there is no server.

01 · EXPORT

Export from Strava

Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → request your archive. Strava emails you a .zip.

02 · DROP

Drop it onto the page

No sign-up, no email, no "connect your account" step. Drag the .zip straight onto Freeroam.

03 · RENDER

Watch it light up

Every route renders into the heatmap. The corridors you travel most glow brightest.

No login · No upload · No server ever sees your routes
